## Scope

The three questions analyse a **completed local achievement of intelligibility at the scale under discussion**.

They are not a universal admission test for whether anything whatsoever is intelligible. A larger matter may remain unresolved while a local matter becomes complete enough for inquiry or action to continue.

## Three dimensions

### Transformation Pattern

The primitive dimension that specifies what kind of unresolvedness is decisively altered between the opening and completed situation.

Question: **What changed?**

### Completion Topology

The primitive dimension that specifies the structural shape by which a Transformation Pattern becomes sufficient as completion.

Question: **What made the change sufficient?**

### Persistence Mode

The primitive dimension that specifies in what mode an achieved completion remains available for subsequent uptake.

Question: **What remained?**

The answers must be discerned independently.

## Six Transformation Patterns

### Differentiation

A Transformation Pattern in which a difference that was unavailable, flattened, conflated, unmarked, or non-operative becomes determinate enough to matter.

### Opening

A Transformation Pattern in which availability, reach, continuation, access, or possibility becomes greater than under the opening constraint.

### Relating

A Transformation Pattern in which a bearing, dependency, ordering, correspondence, reciprocity, response, or mutual determination becomes constitutive of the situation.

### Repair

A Transformation Pattern in which an organization failing relative to an operative criterion is redirected, reweighted, replaced, reversed, or released from the fault.

### Integration

A Transformation Pattern in which parts acquire determinate roles through organization into a whole and thereby gain structure, mutual support, or joint capacity.

### Settlement

A Transformation Pattern in which a live demand for further uptake, choice, explanation, adjudication, completion, or continuation is discharged.

## Three Completion Topologies

### Focal

A Completion Topology in which sufficiency gathers around a privileged locus such as a selected item, source, target, connection, whole, pivot, endpoint, or settlement point.

### Distributed

A Completion Topology in which a constitutive plurality, span, sequence, range, reciprocal field, set of cases, or system belongs to what makes the transformation sufficient.

### Release

A Completion Topology in which completion occurs because something that governed the opening field no longer governs it.

## Three Persistence Modes

### Standing

A Persistence Mode in which the achieved determination itself remains available as the primary residue of completion: “there it is”.

### Bearing

A Persistence Mode in which the achieved result remains what it is only through a constitutive relation, dependency, contrast, source, condition, proportion, frame, support, correspondence, or mutual determination: “it holds through this”.

### Efficacy

A Persistence Mode in which the achieved completion remains as an accomplished capacity, rule, effect, changed continuation, function, or consequential difference in what can happen: “now it works / takes effect”.

## Why there are 54 forms

The current coordinate field is the complete Cartesian product:

**6 Transformations × 3 Completion Topologies × 3 Persistence Modes = 54 forms.**

A manuscript event instantiates a coordinate; it does not create the coordinate.

There is therefore no isolated “form 55” while these three answer sets remain unchanged. A seventh Transformation would regenerate the field as 63. A fourth Completion Topology or Persistence Mode would produce 72. A split or merger among present definitions, failure of axis-independence, or another constitutive dimension would likewise require regeneration of the field.

The current manuscript contains 111 selected event-instantiations. It is not required to instantiate every generated coordinate.

## Coda method

Each chapter contains:

1. `### Three events`
2. three fixed declarative event statements
3. one canonical coordinate and short expression for each event
4. a concise coda gloss
5. `### Five questions`

The pedagogical movement is:

**see first → name second → compose third → compress last**

Looking at the supplied coordinate is allowed. The questions are for making the answer intelligible, not for examination recall.

Practice mode exists for readers who want to reconstruct before revealing the printed coordinate and coda gloss.

## Short expressions

The 54 ultra-short expressions are canonical for the present manuscript and ledger.

They are **not miniature definitions** and should not be revised merely because they do not literally encode all three axes. Their role is under active study as compressed human recognition cues or attractors toward the invariant structure.

Do not modify the short-expression layer, X→Y language, or invariant glosses on the basis of stylistic preference while the current invariant-iteration experiment is pending.

## Experimental / historical material

Historical Sentence Engine material uses a different framing: operator / orientation / closure and X→Y sentence-level completion. Some language from that system is currently being studied as a possible secondary recognition layer beside the present 54 forms.

Historical numerical correspondence does not by itself make historical definitions canonical for the current book.

Current canon is determined by:

1. the 15 definitions above;
2. the 6 × 3 × 3 coordinate rule;
3. the current ledger;
4. the manuscript codas;
5. the current HTML reference system.

## Editing constraints

- Do not force the manuscript to fill all 54 generated coordinates.
- Do not rewrite the 111 fixed event statements merely to make classification easier.
- Do not treat coordinate language as intrinsically ambiguous. If evidence is insufficient, say what is missing.
- Do not collapse local completion into truth, certainty, consensus, finality, or exhaustive resolution.
- Do not turn Practice mode into an exam.
- Do not move reference material back into narrative chapters merely because it is available digitally.
- Preserve the literary architecture unless a concrete gain can be demonstrated.
- When changing any canonical short expression, update every occurrence consistently across ledger, manuscript, and reference surfaces.

## Mechanical invariants to check after edits
