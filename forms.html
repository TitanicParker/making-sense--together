The 54 canonical forms

Choose the independently discerned Transformation, Completion Topology and Persistence Mode; the coordinate resolves to one compact expression.

What “54” means. The 54 are the complete coordinate field generated by 6 Transformations × 3 Completion Topologies × 3 Persistence Modes. A manuscript event can instantiate a coordinate; it does not create the coordinate. The current 111 events test part of the field rather than serving as a quota that must fill every cell.

What could change the field. A seventh Transformation would regenerate it as 7 × 3 × 3 = 63. A fourth Completion Topology or Persistence Mode would produce 72. A split or merger among present definitions, failure of axis-independence, or discovery of another constitutive dimension would likewise require the field to be regenerated. There is therefore no isolated “form 55.”
