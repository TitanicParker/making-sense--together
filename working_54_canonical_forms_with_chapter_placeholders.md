# The 54 Canonical Coordinate Forms

## The three dimensions

**1. Transformation Pattern**

The primitive dimension that specifies what kind of unresolvedness is decisively altered between the opening and completed situation.

**2. Completion Topology**

The primitive dimension that specifies the structural shape by which a Transformation Pattern becomes sufficient as completion.

**3. Persistence Mode**

The primitive dimension that specifies in what mode an achieved completion remains available for subsequent uptake.


## The three questions

1. **What changed?**
   — answered by the **Transformation Pattern**.
2. **What made the change sufficient?**
   — answered by the **Completion Topology**.
3. **How does the achieved result remain available?**
   — answered by the **Persistence Mode**.

That is the exact three-part structure expressed in *Persistence*: a completed form is considered in terms of **what changed, how the change became sufficient, and how the achieved result remains available**.

So in the plainer language we have sometimes used:

> **What changed?**
> **Why was that enough?**
> **What remained?**

Those are the three questions every completed intelligibility answers.

## The six Transformation Patterns

**4. Differentiation**

A Transformation Pattern in which a difference that was unavailable, flattened, conflated, unmarked, or non-operative becomes determinate enough to matter.

**5. Opening**

A Transformation Pattern in which availability, reach, continuation, access, or possibility becomes greater than under the opening constraint.

**6. Relating**

A Transformation Pattern in which a bearing, dependency, ordering, correspondence, reciprocity, response, or mutual determination becomes constitutive of the situation.

**7. Repair**

A Transformation Pattern in which an organization failing relative to an operative criterion is redirected, reweighted, replaced, reversed, or released from the fault.

**8. Integration**

A Transformation Pattern in which parts acquire determinate roles through organization into a whole and thereby gain structure, mutual support, or joint capacity.

**9. Settlement**

A Transformation Pattern in which a live demand for further uptake, choice, explanation, adjudication, completion, or continuation is discharged.

## The three Completion Topologies

**10. Focal**

A Completion Topology in which sufficiency gathers around a privileged locus such as a selected item, source, target, connection, whole, pivot, endpoint, or settlement point.

**11. Distributed**

A Completion Topology in which a constitutive plurality, span, sequence, range, reciprocal field, set of cases, or system belongs to what makes the transformation sufficient.

**12. Release**

A Completion Topology in which completion occurs because something that governed the opening field no longer governs it.

## The three Persistence Modes

**13. Standing**

A Persistence Mode in which the achieved determination itself remains available as the primary residue of completion: “there it is”.

**14. Bearing**

A Persistence Mode in which the achieved result remains what it is only through a constitutive relation, dependency, contrast, source, condition, proportion, frame, support, correspondence, or mutual determination: “it holds through this”.

**15. Efficacy**

A Persistence Mode in which the achieved completion remains as an accomplished capacity, rule, effect, changed continuation, function, or consequential difference in what can happen: “now it works / takes effect”.

## A. Differentiation

### Focal × Standing
One decisive contrast establishes which is which.

### Focal × Bearing
One decisive contrast establishes how two things differ in relation.

### Focal × Efficacy
One decisive distinction enables a new move.

### Distributed × Standing
Several contrasts together establish a stable distinction.

### Distributed × Bearing
Several cues together clarify the relational difference.

### Distributed × Efficacy
Several distinctions together enable competent continuation.

### Release × Standing
A former conflation loses its hold, leaving a distinction established.

### Release × Bearing
A misleading way of grouping things loses authority, revealing their actual bearing.

### Release × Efficacy
An old confusion stops governing action, allowing a new way forward.

---

## B. Opening

### Focal × Standing
One decisive event establishes that a possibility is now open.

### Focal × Bearing
One decisive event opens a new relation or route among things.

### Focal × Efficacy
One decisive event gives direct access to a new action.

### Distributed × Standing
Several conditions together establish that a possibility is available.

### Distributed × Bearing
Several conditions together open a new relational position.

### Distributed × Efficacy
Several conditions together make a new capability usable.

### Release × Standing
A blocking condition loses its claim, establishing that the way is open.

### Release × Bearing
An old constraint falls away, changing how the available possibilities stand to one another.

### Release × Efficacy
A former barrier ceases to govern, and action becomes possible.

---

## C. Relating

### Focal × Standing
One decisive link establishes how two things are connected.

### Focal × Bearing
One decisive link leaves a relation available as the main result.

### Focal × Efficacy
One decisive relation enables a new action or inference.

### Distributed × Standing
Several connections together establish a relational conclusion.

### Distributed × Bearing
Several relations jointly make the pattern itself intelligible.

### Distributed × Efficacy
A network of relations jointly enables effective continuation.

### Release × Standing
A mistaken relation loses authority, leaving a corrected connection established.

### Release × Bearing
An old relational assumption falls away, allowing the actual relation to carry the result.

### Release × Efficacy
A misleading connection stops governing, enabling a different practical route.

---

## D. Repair

### Focal × Standing
One decisive correction restores adequate order and establishes the repaired state.

### Focal × Bearing
One decisive correction restores the right relation among parts.

### Focal × Efficacy
One decisive correction restores functioning.

### Distributed × Standing
Several corrections together establish that the thing is adequately put right.

### Distributed × Bearing
Several repaired relations together restore the proper arrangement.

### Distributed × Efficacy
Several repairs jointly restore usable functioning.

### Release × Standing
A condition that kept the thing in failure loses its grip, leaving it adequately restored.

### Release × Bearing
A distorting or obstructing condition falls away, restoring the required relation.

### Release × Efficacy
A governing fault or obstruction ceases to control the system, and functioning returns.

---

## E. Integration

### Focal × Standing
One decisive organizing principle makes the whole intelligible as one.

### Focal × Bearing
One decisive organizing relation gives the parts their places in the whole.

### Focal × Efficacy
One decisive organizing move makes the whole function.

### Distributed × Standing
Several parts together establish one coherent whole.

### Distributed × Bearing
The whole exists primarily in the mutual relations among its parts.

### Distributed × Efficacy
Several coordinated parts together make a functioning whole.

### Release × Standing
A fragmentation-producing constraint falls away, allowing the whole to stand as one.

### Release × Bearing
An old division loses authority, allowing the parts to take up a new relational whole.

### Release × Efficacy
A former incompatibility ceases to govern, allowing coordinated functioning.

---

## F. Settlement

### Focal × Standing
One decisive answer settles the question and remains established.

### Focal × Bearing
One decisive answer settles how the matter stands in relation to something else.

### Focal × Efficacy
One decisive answer lets action continue.

### Distributed × Standing
Several considerations together settle the question.

### Distributed × Bearing
Several considerations together settle how the matter bears on the wider situation.

### Distributed × Efficacy
Several considerations together make the matter settled enough to proceed.

### Release × Standing
The demand for an answer ceases because its governing basis has fallen away, leaving a settled status.

### Release × Bearing
The old question loses control, changing how the matter now stands in the wider situation.

### Release × Efficacy
The question no longer governs the course ahead, and life or action can continue differently.
