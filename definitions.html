A lookup table for the three questions, the three dimensions, and the twelve definitions that answer them. Start with the event; use the table to decide what to notice.

Scope. These questions concern a completed local achievement of intelligibility at the scale under discussion: something has become different, that difference has become sufficient for the live matter, and the achieved result is available in some mode for what follows.

Discern the three answers independently. Knowing what changed does not tell you what made that change sufficient; neither answer tells you how the achieved result remains available. What changed? → Transformation · What made it sufficient? → Completion · What remained? → Persistence
