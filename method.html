Scope

The system concerns a completed local achievement of intelligibility at the scale under discussion. A larger matter can remain unresolved while something local becomes clear enough, settled enough, repaired enough, or otherwise complete enough for inquiry or action to continue.

Sense here is not identified with truth, certainty, consensus, exhaustive explanation, or finality. An intelligible achievement may later be questioned, corrected, forgotten, taught, inherited, revised, or used as material for a larger inquiry.

How far upstream does the phenomenon go?

The present claim is stronger than a merely linguistic one, but narrower than a claim of biological universality.

Developmental and comparative evidence supports a stripped-down architecture of situated intelligibility before productive language and outside the human species: an organism can encounter an action-relevant insufficiency, reorganize its engagement, reach a locally adequate basis for proceeding or withholding action, and retain a change that affects later engagement.

Especially informative cases include infant locomotor-affordance learning, in which what becomes available depends on the relation among body, posture, surface and slope; New Caledonian crows constructing compound tools from individually inadequate parts; and great apes abandoning previously successful methods when altered physical constraints make them obsolete.

Current evidential boundary. This does not establish that the six Transformation Patterns, three Completion Topologies, three Persistence Modes, or all 54 crossings are evolutionarily conserved natural kinds. It supports the more modest claim that the minimal architecture the book studies is not dependent on productive language.

Language and culture appear to amplify rather than simply originate this architecture. They make distinctions nameable, revisitable, transmissible, combinable and preservable across persons and generations. This is the sense in which the project now uses the informal term earthliness: the possibility that the basic phenomenon belongs to situated creatures dealing with a consequential world before it belongs to any one language, discipline or culture.

Intellectual precedents and the present claim

The project does not claim that situated transitions into intelligibility were wholly unnoticed before this book. Important precedents include Dewey's transformation of indeterminate situations through inquiry; Wittgenstein's attack on understanding as a private mental occurrence and his emphasis on being able to go on; Weick's account of sensemaking as sufficient plausible organization for continued action; and Dervin's structural decomposition of situated sense-making episodes.

The stronger claim is one of recombination and internal differentiation: the book treats locally completed intelligibility transitions as a common comparative unit across practical, social, mathematical, interpretive, embodied and material cases, and separates three questions that neighbouring traditions commonly leave entangled—what changed, what made it sufficient, and how the achieved result remains available afterward.

The three-dimensional formalization is therefore best treated as a proposal to be tested, not as a declaration that the 54 have been proved universal.

The three questions

Every completed form is considered in terms of three independently discerned answers:

QuestionDimensionWhat it specifies
What changed?Transformation PatternThe kind of unresolvedness decisively altered.
What made the change sufficient?Completion TopologyThe structural shape by which that change became enough.
What remained?Persistence ModeThe mode in which the achieved result remains available.

The answer to one question does not determine either of the others. The definitions page gives the exact canonical wording for all fifteen terms.

Why there are 54 forms

The present field crosses six Transformation Patterns with three Completion Topologies and three Persistence Modes:

6 × 3 × 3 = 54

An event instantiates a coordinate; it does not create the coordinate. There is therefore no isolated “55th form” while the present axes and values remain unchanged. A new value, a split or merger of a present value, failure of axis-independence, or another constitutive dimension would regenerate the field rather than append one miscellaneous box.

How the book uses the system

Each chapter works first as a chapter. The coda then identifies three local achievements of intelligibility and gives each a coordinate and compact expression. Five questions invite the reader to reconstruct what changed, what made it enough, and what remained.

Pedagogical movement: see it first → name it second → compose it third → compress it last. Looking at the supplied answer is allowed; the questions are for discernment, not examination recall.

The optional Practice mode in the reader hides answer-bearing coordinate material and explanatory coda glosses until the reader chooses to compare.

What the ultra-short expressions are

The 54 canonical ultra-short expressions are not miniature definitions. The coordinate supplies the formal distinction. The short expression supplies a compressed human cue toward recognition.

The expressions can therefore be individually underdetermining without making the forms themselves indistinct. A useful formulation is:

The openness belongs to the language; the distinction belongs to the form.

The 54 canonical expressions are frozen for the present edition.

Blind recognition and cue ablation

An internal model experiment tested whether three linguistic layers could lead a reasoner back to the canonical coordinate when the coordinate itself was withheld:

  1. the canonical ultra-short expression;
  2. an X→Y schematic sentence inherited from earlier work;
  3. an ordinary-language house-rule sentence.

With all three cues present, all 54 coordinates were recovered exactly in that test.

The pattern suggests complementarity rather than redundancy: the compressed expressions carry different parts of the recoverable structure, and their conjunction can make the invariant more determinate than any one layer alone.

Limit. These are internal semantic-robustness experiments. They do not establish that every human reader will classify every case identically, that every generated coordinate must occur in ordinary life, or that model recovery proves the ontology of the field.

The empirical frontier

The strongest scientific objection to the present framework is mechanistic underdetermination. Similar functional shapes—difficulty, reorganization, successful stopping, retention—can be produced by different mechanisms such as reinforcement learning, motor adaptation, perceptual attunement, inhibitory control, causal inference or social learning.

The framework therefore becomes empirically stronger only if its distinctions predict new dissociations. Examples of relevant tests include whether Standing, Bearing and Efficacy differ in transfer or vulnerability to changed context; whether Focal, Distributed and Release completions respond differently when decisive components or governing constraints are altered; and whether Transformation classes remain distinguishable when endpoint and reward are held constant.

The book's position. Making Sense Together proposes a morphology of intelligible completion. It does not yet claim a single mechanism shared by every instance. The next scientific burden is to discover whether the morphology predicts differences that independent experiments can detect.

The semantic-field exercise

A later generation exercise asked for ten different 3–5 word attractors for each of the 54 cells. The aim was not to improve or replace the canonical expressions, but to see whether distinct ordinary-language entrances could repeatedly occupy the same structural region.

The exercise produced a clear compositional pattern:

  • Transformation contributes the kind of sense being altered: difference, possibility, connection, repair, whole, settlement.
  • Completion contributes the shape of enoughness: a point, a field, or release from an earlier governing condition.
  • Persistence contributes what remains available: a standing result, a result held through relation, or a changed capacity for what follows.

Some generated phrases were natural and vivid; others exposed the machinery too visibly or became linguistically strained. That boundary is useful. Formal composability does not by itself guarantee good ordinary language.

Public presentation. The site therefore publishes a curated matrix of five attractors for each coordinate rather than the raw spreadsheet or all ten generated candidates. The matrix is meant to give a palpable feel for the semantic basin around each invariant while preserving the canonical expression as canonical.

Editorial status

The architecture is stable. The literary manuscript, coda structure, three axes, fifteen definitions, and 54 canonical ultra-short expressions are fixed for the present edition unless new evidence demonstrates a substantive problem.

Current work is therefore limited to proofing, interaction checks, publication packaging, and clearly separated experiments.

Mechanical checks

A healthy build has 37 chapters, 37 “Three events” sections, 37 “Five questions” sections, 111 numbered coda event statements, 111 coordinate lines, and 54 canonical short expressions. Coordinate numbers must agree with their Transformation × Completion × Persistence positions, and manuscript short expressions must agree with the canonical ledger.
